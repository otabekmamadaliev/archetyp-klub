About

A club where people stay

Rogatka has been on Pocieszka for over a decade. We started with one adult group; today we run five groups across five age brackets.

Not everyone who comes here wants to compete — and that is fine. Most train because they enjoy it, because they want to be in shape, and because they sleep better afterwards.
